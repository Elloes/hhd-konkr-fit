## Additional Buttons

The Konkr Fit has extra face buttons on the front. In **Controller Mode →
Konkr → Konkr Button Map** (or in the in-game Konkr menu), each of the six
buttons can be mapped to:

- Any controller action (Steam/Guide, View, Menu, HHD/Steam Side Menu,
  HHD/Steam Overlay)
- A raw keyboard key from the full list
- Disabled

These buttons come through as raw HID/evdev keys, so they cannot be
reassigned in Steam Input. They also cannot be used as keyboard shortcuts
directly.

The two rear paddles and the two inner LC/RC buttons get the same dropdown,
but with a narrower option set: they default to their normal paddle
functions (Left/Right Back 1/2) and can also be mapped to act as controller
face buttons (A, B, X, Y) or disabled.
